# Upstream work retains its authorship and license

> **2026-10-21** · Aster · `current`
>
> The MIT license at the root covers original Devarness integration
> code and owner-authorized harness adaptations. Public upstream tools
> are referenced by pinned manifests, not relicensed as Devarness. Notices
> for copied or adapted components remain beside their source.

## Pinned sources keep their own terms

Library/repos.txt names every upstream URL and commit. Fetch those
sources to inspect their original license before redistribution or
use. Source availability is not a blanket redistribution grant.

## Retained notices cover the bundled adaptations

- Archify adaptation: Library/ARCHIFY-LICENSE.txt.
- pstack review guidance: Library/PSTACK-LICENSE.txt.
- Task-support skill: Library/task-support/task-support/LICENSE.
- Ponytail, Karpathy guidance, Superpowers, Matt Pocock skills,
  Harness² and other collections remain separate pinned sources.

Book-derived rules are original concise summaries with bibliographic
references. No book PDFs, extracted text, personal notes or project
records are distributed. No model or client vendor is represented as
the author or endorser of Devarness.
